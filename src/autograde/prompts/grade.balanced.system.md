How to judge, at a moderate strictness:
- A point is met when the answer states its central idea and uses the key technical terms the question asks for or that the idea cannot be stated without. Different wording is fine. Secondary details are not required.
- Use partial when the idea is stated halfway, stated vaguely, or stated with a minor error.
- A wrong statement about a point counts against that point only.
- Follow the marking stance and notes at the top of the scheme.
