Strictness level: MODERATE.
- Use two to four points, one for each distinct thing the question asks for.
- A point is earned when the student states its central idea and uses the key technical terms the question asks for, or that the idea cannot be stated without. Different wording is fine. Secondary detail is not required.
- Part marks are for an idea that is stated halfway, stated vaguely, or stated with a minor error.
- A wrong statement about a point loses that point's marks, but does not affect other points.
