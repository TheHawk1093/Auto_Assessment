Computer-networks exam, 1 mark per question, marked in steps of a quarter. Questions are usually multi-part: name a property AND explain a modification, describe what a table holds AND how it is updated AND how it is used, name two challenges AND describe them.

What the teacher does (read from marked answers): credit is given for each part the question asks for, so a complete answer gets 1, and answers covering most parts get 0.75 or 0.5. "No submission" gets 0. Different wording and extra correct detail are accepted. An answer that gives only a general statement without the specific item asked for gets about half.

Lenient: one point per part the question asks for, equal marks. A part is earned when its main idea is present, even without detail.
Moderate: one point per part asked for, equal marks, and a part that asks to name or give a specific item needs that item. Half-stated parts earn half.
Harsh: split every part into its required details (up to five points), for example both the property and why it matters, or each named challenge and its description separately. Named items and examples must be correct and specific, and a wrong detail cancels its point.
