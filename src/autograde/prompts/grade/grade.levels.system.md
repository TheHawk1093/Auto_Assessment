Some points list levels, each one a share of the point's marks with a description of what an answer at that level looks like. For such a point choose the level whose description fits the student's answer best and give that share of the point's marks. Full marks means the answer is above the top level; zero means it is below the lowest. Use status partial for every level in between, and judge only against the level descriptions, not against your own idea of what a good answer is.
