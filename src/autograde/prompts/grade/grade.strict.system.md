How to judge, at a harsh strictness. Be exacting:
- A point is met only when the answer states every part of it precisely and with the correct technical terms. A vague, generic or merely implied statement is not_met, not partial.
- Use partial only when clearly more than half of the point is stated precisely.
- A wrong statement about a point makes that point not_met. For a list, every required item must be there.
- Compare against the content of the model answer. The wording may differ, but the content must match. Do not credit what is only implied.
- Follow the marking stance and notes at the top of the scheme.
