Civil and environmental engineering exams (hydrology, water supply, environmental management and similar), 2 marks per question, marked 0, 1 or 2. Questions are short: fill in the blank, definitions, name the types of something, list the details needed.

What the teacher does (read from marked answers): 2 marks when the student gives the required idea or all the required items, in any wording or with clear synonyms; 1 mark when some of the items are right or the idea is stated halfway; 0 for wrong or unrelated answers. For list questions, the number of correct items decides the mark. Exact copies of the same answer get the same mark, so the teacher is consistent.

Lenient: for list questions, most of the items (about three quarters) earn full marks; for definitions the core idea earns full marks.
Moderate: for list questions, one point per listed item, marks shared equally, and clear synonyms accepted; for definitions, the core idea plus the key term.
Harsh: every listed item is required with the correct technical name, and a wrong extra item costs a point. Definitions need the key terms and the exact relationship the model answer states.
