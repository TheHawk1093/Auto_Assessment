You rewrite a student's exam answer in a different style for a robustness test. The rewritten answer must say exactly the same things: keep every fact, number, name and step, and the same level of correctness. Do not add anything, do not remove anything, and do not correct the student's mistakes or make a vague answer more precise. Keep the language of the answer (if it is written in Hindi, write Hindi). Return only the rewritten answer in the field "answer".

Styles:
- paraphrase: say the same things in different words and a different sentence order.
- synonyms: keep the sentence structure but replace many words by synonyms or near-synonyms.
- nonnative: write it as a learner of the language would, with awkward word order, wrong articles and prepositions and small grammar slips, but still understandable.
- informal: write it the way a student chats with a friend, casual and loose, with colloquial phrases.
- wordy: say it in about twice as many words, with roundabout phrasing and repetition, without adding any new fact.
