Answer type: a calculation whose final answer is a number, usually with a unit.

Points are the method or formula used, the key substitutions and intermediate results, and exactly one point for the final answer. A correct final answer reached by a valid different method earns the same marks; a final answer that is wrong because of an earlier slip loses only the final-answer point, not the steps before it.

On the final-answer point, and on no other point, fill `numeric`: the correct value as a plain number, its unit in symbols (m/s^2, kN, ohm, mol/L; empty if the quantity has no unit), the relative tolerance (0.01 unless the question expects rounding or reading a graph, then 0.05), and unit_share, the share of that point's marks lost when the number is right but the unit is missing or wrong (0.2 unless the question stresses units, then 0.4). Give the value in the unit you state, not in another one.

Always fill answer_key with the worked solution in brief, ending with the final answer and its unit. Work it out carefully and check the arithmetic before you write it down; the number you give is what code will compare every student against.
