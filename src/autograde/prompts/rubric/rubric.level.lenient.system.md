Strictness level: LENIENT.
- Use as few points as the question needs, usually one to three.
- A point is earned when the student states its central idea in any wording, however informal or brief. Technical terms are not required. Missing secondary detail costs nothing.
- Part marks are for an answer that is on the right track but vague or incomplete. Be generous with them.
- A wrong or irrelevant extra remark does not cost marks unless it contradicts the central idea itself.
