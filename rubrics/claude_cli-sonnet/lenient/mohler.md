# mohler: lenient rubrics

Marks per point add up to the question total.

## mohler/E01.Q01 (5 marks)

**Question:** What is the role of a prototype program in problem solving?

**Model answer:** To simulate the behaviour of portions of the desired software product.

_Marking stance: lenient. Full marks for saying in any wording that a prototype simulates, models or imitates how parts of the final software will behave. Give part marks for vague or incomplete answers, such as a trial, sample or early version, or a way to test ideas, that don't say it mimics the product's behaviour. Give no marks for answers about something unrelated. Extra correct detail does no harm._

- **P1** (5): States that a prototype program simulates/imitates/mimics (or gives a working model or preview of) the behaviour of parts of the intended software product. _Partial: Award about 2-3 marks for a related but incomplete statement, e.g. it is a test or trial version, a sample or early version of the software, or helps check ideas, without saying it mimics the behaviour of the final product or its parts._

## mohler/E05.Q01 (5 marks)

**Question:** In one sentence, what is the main idea implemented by insertion sort?

**Model answer:** Taking one array element at a time, from left to right, it inserts it in the right position among the already sorted elements on its left.

_Marking stance: lenient. Full marks for stating, in any wording, that each element in turn is inserted into its correct place within the already sorted part of the array. Brief or informal answers are fine, and 'left to right' or exact terms are not required. Part marks for a vague or half-correct idea, such as only 'inserts in the right position' or 'builds a sorted section'. No marks for answers describing other sorts, such as repeated swapping of neighbours or finding the minimum._

- **P1** (5): States the core idea: elements are taken one at a time and each is inserted into its correct position among the already sorted elements (the sorted part, on its left). _Partial: Award 2-3 marks if the answer only says it inserts or places elements in the right position, or only says it builds a sorted portion, without linking the two._


## mohler/E05.Q02 (5 marks)

**Question:** In one sentence, what is the main idea implemented by selection sort?

**Model answer:** Taking one array element at a time, from left to right, it identifies the minimum from the remaining elements and swaps it with the current element.

_Marking stance: lenient. Full marks for stating, in any wording, that selection sort repeatedly finds the minimum of the remaining elements and swaps/places it at the current position. Part marks for an answer with only half of this (e.g. just 'finds the smallest element' or 'swaps elements into place') or a vague but on-track description. No marks for descriptions of other sorts (e.g. adjacent swaps, inserting into a sorted part) or unrelated statements._

- **P1** (5): States the core idea: repeatedly find the minimum (smallest) element among the remaining/unsorted elements and swap/place it into the current position, moving left to right. _Partial: Award 2–3 marks if only part is given, e.g. says it finds the smallest element or sorts by selecting elements one at a time, but omits the swap/placing at the current position (or vice versa)._
