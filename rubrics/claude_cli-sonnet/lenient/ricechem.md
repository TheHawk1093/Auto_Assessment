## ricechem/Q1 (8 marks)

**Question:** When studying the emission sources within the Milky Way, a satellite detected interplanetary clouds containing silicon atoms that have lost five electrons.
b) The ionization energies corresponding to the removal of the third, fourth, and fifth electrons in silicon are 3231, 4356, and 16091 kJ/mol, respectively. 
Using core charge calculations and your understanding of Coulomb's Law, briefly explain 1) why the removal of each additional electron requires more energy than the removal of the previous one, and 2) the relative magnitude of the values observed.
This question can be answered reasonably in around 150 words or fewer.

**Model answer:** A full-mark answer contains all of the following:
- correctly cites decreased electron electron repulsion (1 marks)
- relates decreased electron electron repulsion to decreased potential energy (1 marks)
- 3rd and 4th electrons ionized feel same core charge (1 marks)
- 3rd and 4th electrons ionized from n=3 shell and have same radius (1 marks)
- 5th electron ionized from n=2 shell and feels higher core charge (1 marks)
- 5th electron ionized from n=2 shell and has smaller radius (1 marks)
- correctly explains relationship of potential energy to ionization energy (2 marks)

_Marking stance: lenient. Full marks need the four ideas in any wording: less repulsion lowers potential energy, 3rd and 4th electrons are in the same shell with the same core charge and radius, the 5th is in an inner shell with higher core charge and smaller radius, and lower potential energy means higher ionization energy. Give part marks for answers that are on the right track but vague or incomplete, and accept implied links. Restating the question or wrong extra remarks cost nothing unless they contradict the central idea._

- **P1** (2): Says that removing each electron leaves fewer electrons, so there is less electron-electron repulsion, and links this to lower potential energy (the remaining electrons are held more tightly / are more stable). An implied link is enough. _Partial: 1 mark if only decreased electron-electron repulsion is stated, with no link to potential energy_
- **P2** (2): The 3rd and 4th electrons come from the same shell (n=3), so they have about the same radius and feel the same core charge. This is why their ionization energies (3231 and 4356) are similar and fairly close. _Partial: 1 mark if only same shell/radius or only same core charge is stated_
- **P3** (2): The 5th electron comes from an inner shell (n=2), so it has a smaller radius and feels a higher core charge. This gives a much larger ionization energy (16091). _Partial: 1 mark if only the inner shell/smaller radius or only the higher core charge is stated_
- **P4** (2): Explains with Coulomb's law that stronger attraction (higher core charge, smaller distance) means lower potential energy for the electron, so more energy is needed to remove it. That is, higher ionization energy goes with lower potential energy. _Partial: 1 mark if only partly stated, e.g. stronger attraction means more energy is needed, with no link to potential energy_
