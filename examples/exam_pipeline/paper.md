# Strength of Materials: Mid-Semester Test

Time: 60 minutes. Maximum marks: 25.

Instructions:
- Answer any FIVE of the SIX questions. If you answer all six, only your best five count.
- For numerical questions, show your working. An answer that gives only a number, with no working, earns no marks.
- Marks for each question are shown in brackets. Half marks may be given.

**Q1.** Distinguish between stress and strain, and state Hooke's law. [5 marks]

**Q2.** A steel rod of diameter 20 mm carries a tensile load of 50 kN. Calculate the stress in the rod, in MPa. [5 marks]

**Q3.** (a) Calculate the cross-sectional area of a circular bar of diameter 12 mm. [2 marks]
(b) Using your answer to (a), find the elongation of this bar, 2 m long, under a tensile load of 20 kN. Take E = 200 GPa. [3 marks]

**Q4.** Describe a tensile test on a specimen of mild steel. Explain what each region of the stress-strain curve tells you. [5 marks]

**Q5.** Show that the hoop stress in a thin-walled cylinder of internal diameter d and wall thickness t, under internal pressure p, is σ = pd/(2t). [5 marks]

**Q6.** Why do engineers prefer ductile materials for structures that may be overloaded? Give two reasons and one example. [5 marks]
