Q3(d). Advantage of the observer: it uses the plant model and the applied input u, so its velocity
estimate has (almost) no phase lag; the 20 Hz filter on differenced data always delays by about
1/(2π fc) ≈ 8 ms. It can also reconstruct states that cannot be obtained by differencing (e.g.
disturbance, when augmented).
Fliter is safer when the model is uncertain or changes (unknown payload, friction, contact, actuator
saturation): the observer then gives a biased or poorly-behaved estimat while the model-free filter
still follows the measured data.
