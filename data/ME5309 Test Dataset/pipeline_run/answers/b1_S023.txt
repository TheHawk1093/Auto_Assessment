Q3(d).
An observer uses the plant model and known control input to estimate unmeasured states such
as velocity, and its poles can be designed. A simple low-pass filter cannot exploit the state-space
model in this way. A filter may be safer when the model is poorly known or unreliable, because
it relies less on model accuracy.
The observer uses the plant model and the measured output to reconstruct the unmeasured
state. Its convergence depends on the model and the observer poles. A low-pass filter is simpler ,
but generally introduces phase lag and does not explicitly use the plant dynamics.
